About

I like figuring out how things work.
Then making them work better.

I’m drawn to messy problems — the kind where the answer isn’t obvious and figuring out what to do is only half the work. I like bringing structure to them, getting into the details, and staying close enough to see an idea through.

Lately, that curiosity has pulled me toward building. AI has made it possible to go much further with an idea on my own, and I’ve been learning by making things — starting with Musee and seeing where the next idea takes me.
